# Research notes (`research/` folders)

Loaded when a file inside a `research/` folder is read or written.

- Every claim carries its source: URL or file path, and the date you read it. No source, no claim.
- Separate what the source says from what you conclude. Conclusions first, sources under them.
- Mark what you could not confirm UNVERIFIED rather than dropping or smoothing it.
- Numbers keep the source's precision and its denominator. A rate without its base is not a finding.
- Quotes under 15 words, in quotation marks, attributed. A summary is shorter than and different from the source.
- One question per file. Name the file after the question, date it, put the answer in the first paragraph.
